# Daily Development Logs

## Purpose

Track daily progress, decisions, issues, and solutions. This is your professional development journal.

---

## Structure

```
docs/DAILY_LOGS/
├── README.md (this file)
├── DAY_01.md
├── DAY_02.md
├── DAY_03.md
├── ...
└── SUMMARY.md (weekly/monthly summaries)
```

---

## Daily Log Template

Each day should have:
1. **Date & Day Number**
2. **Goals for Today**
3. **What Was Accomplished**
4. **Issues Encountered**
5. **Solutions Found**
6. **Decisions Made**
7. **Code Changes** (summary)
8. **Next Steps**
9. **Time Spent**
10. **Screenshots/Notes** (if applicable)

---

## How to Use

1. **Start of Day:** Create/update the day's log with goals
2. **During Development:** Note issues and solutions as they happen
3. **End of Day:** Fill in accomplishments, decisions, next steps
4. **Reference:** When stuck, look back at similar issues from previous days

---

## Benefits

- ✅ Track progress over time
- ✅ Reference solutions to past problems
- ✅ Understand why decisions were made
- ✅ Identify patterns in issues
- ✅ Estimate future tasks better
- ✅ Debug faster by looking at what changed
