# Target Audience-Focused Research: Consumer Features Only

**Date:** 2025-12-29  
**Target Audience:** Families, Students, International Students, International Employees, Roommates, Couples, Travelers

**Purpose:** Filtered research focusing only on features and patterns relevant to our consumer target audience. Corporate/enterprise features removed.

---

## Executive Summary

This document contains **curated research** focusing only on consumer-facing apps and features relevant to our target audience:
- **Families** (chore management, family coordination)
- **Students** (expense splitting, shared living)
- **International Students/Employees** (multi-currency, home + local finances)
- **Roommates** (expense splitting, chores, shared living management)
- **Couples** (shared expenses, joint finances)
- **Travelers** (expense splitting, multi-currency, offline capabilities)

**Removed from Focus:**
- ❌ Corporate expense management (SAP Concur, corporate Ramp, enterprise Expensify features)
- ❌ Business-focused features (approval workflows, corporate card integrations, policy enforcement)
- ❌ Enterprise-only solutions

## Category 1: Split Expense Apps (P2P/Social) - PRIMARY FOCUS

### 1.1 Splitwise ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Why It's Relevant:** Perfect for roommates, students, travelers, groups

**Core Features We Need:**
- ✅ Expense creation and splitting (equal, custom, percentage)
- ✅ **Debt Simplification** - **CRITICAL FEATURE** (reduces multiple debts to minimal transactions)
- ✅ Settlement tracking and flow
- ✅ Groups for recurring expenses (roommates, travel groups)
- ✅ Receipt attachments
- ✅ Multi-currency support
- ✅ Payment reminders
- ✅ Offline expense entry

**UI/UX Patterns:**
- ✅ Clean, minimal interface
- ✅ Visual balance display (who owes whom) - **Primary screen**
- ✅ "Simplify debt" button prominently displayed
- ✅ Color-coded balances (green = owed to you, red = you owe)
- ✅ Group-based navigation

**Key Learnings:**
- **Debt simplification algorithm is critical** - Users love this feature
- Visual balance display should be the main screen focus
- Offline capability is important for travelers

**What Users Love:**
- Debt simplification saves time
- Great for groups and recurring expenses
- Reliable and accurate

**What Users Don't Like:**
- Free version limitations
- UI feels outdated
- Limited payment integration

---

### 1.2 Venmo ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Why It's Relevant:** Popular among students, social payment standard

**Core Features We Need:**
- ✅ P2P payments (instant)
- ✅ Social feed (with privacy controls) - **Engagement driver**
- ✅ Payment requests
- ✅ Split payments
- ✅ QR code payments
- ✅ Payment links

**Key Learnings:**
- **Social elements increase engagement** - Makes payments fun
- QR codes essential for in-person payments
- Privacy controls critical (social feed must be opt-in/private by default)

**What Users Love:**
- Fast payments
- Fun social aspect
- Easy to use

**What Users Don't Like:**
- Privacy concerns with social feed

**Note for Our App:** We can learn from social engagement but prioritize privacy more than Venmo

---

### 1.3 Tricount ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Why It's Relevant:** BEST for travelers and international users

**Core Features We Need:**
- ✅ Multi-currency with real-time conversion - **CRITICAL for our international audience**
- ✅ Offline mode (full functionality) - **ESSENTIAL for travelers**
- ✅ Expense tracking and splitting
- ✅ Group expenses
- ✅ Settlement suggestions
- ✅ Statistics and analytics
- ✅ Receipt photos

**Key Learnings:**
- **Multi-currency is non-negotiable** for international students/employees
- **Offline mode is critical** - Many users travel without reliable internet
- Travel-focused design works well for our target audience

**What Users Love:**
- Excellent multi-currency handling
- Works offline (perfect for travel)
- Great for group travel expenses

**What Users Don't Like:**
- UI could be more modern

**Priority for Our App:** ⚠️ **HIGH PRIORITY** - Multi-currency and offline mode are essential

---

### 1.4 Settle Up ⭐⭐⭐

**Why It's Relevant:** Good debt optimization, useful for roommates/groups

**Core Features We Need:**
- ✅ Debt optimization (minimizes transactions)
- ✅ Multiple currencies
- ✅ Expense splitting

**Key Learnings:**
- Debt optimization is important but Splitwise does it better
- Multi-currency support is standard requirement

---

### 1.5 Splid, Cash App, Pigeon, Cumbuca

**Why They're Less Relevant:**
- Splid: Simple but less feature-rich
- Cash App: More payment-focused, less expense splitting
- Pigeon: Basic features, limited adoption
- Cumbuca: Region-specific (Brazil)

**Takeaway:** Focus on Splitwise and Tricount patterns, learn social elements from Venmo

## Key Patterns for Our Target Audience

### 1. Multi-Currency Support ⚠️ **ESSENTIAL**

**Why:** International students and employees need to manage:
- Local currency (where they study/work)
- Home country currency (where family/money is)

**From Tricount:**
- Real-time currency conversion
- Support for multiple currencies in same group
- Historical rates for past transactions

**Our Implementation:**
- Primary currency setting per user
- Support multiple currencies in expenses
- Convert and display in primary currency
- Track both local and home finances

---

### 2. Offline Mode ⚠️ **ESSENTIAL FOR TRAVELERS**

**Why:** Travelers often lack reliable internet

**From Tricount:**
- Full functionality without internet
- Syncs when connection available
- No data loss

**Our Implementation Priority:**
- Core features work offline
- Queue actions for sync
- Offline-first architecture

---

### 3. Debt Simplification ⚠️ **CRITICAL**

**Why:** Groups with many expenses create complex debt webs

**From Splitwise:**
- Graph algorithm to minimize transaction count
- Reduces 10+ transactions to 2-3
- Users love this feature

**Our Implementation Priority:**
- HIGH - Must implement debt simplification algorithm
- Visual "Simplify" button
- Show before/after transaction count

---

### 4. Gamification for Chores ⚠️ **HIGH PRIORITY**

**Why:** Increases engagement and completion rates significantly

**From OurHome:**
- Points system motivates users
- Works for kids and adults
- Increases completion rates

**Our Implementation:**
- Points for chore completion
- Streaks for consistency
- Visual progress tracking
- Leaderboards (optional, privacy-conscious)

---

### 5. Visual Progress Tracking ⚠️ **MEDIUM PRIORITY**

**Why:** More engaging than text lists

**From Tody:**
- Visual cleanliness indicators
- Room-based organization
- Beautiful, motivating design

**Our Implementation:**
- Visual chore completion status
- Progress bars for recurring chores
- Color-coded status indicators

---

### 6. All-in-One Integration ⚠️ **OUR CORE DIFFERENTIATOR**

**From Flatastic:**
- Proves all-in-one works
- Reduces app switching
- Integrates expenses + chores + communication

**Our Advantage:**
- We go further: Expenses + Chores + Listings + Finance + Trust Scores
- Better UI/UX than Flatastic
- More comprehensive integration

---

### 7. Social Elements (Privacy-First) ⚠️ **MEDIUM PRIORITY**

**From Venmo:**
- Social feed increases engagement
- Makes app more fun

**Our Approach:**
- Learn engagement patterns
- But prioritize privacy more than Venmo
- Opt-in social features only
- Trust scores visible (transparency)

## Key Takeaways for Our App

### What Makes Us Different:

1. **All-in-One:** Expenses + Chores + Listings + Finance + Trust Scores (more than Flatastic)
2. **Multi-Currency:** Essential for international audience (like Tricount)
3. **Trust Scores:** Unique across all activities (no competitor does this)
4. **Privacy-First Social:** Social elements with better privacy than Venmo
5. **Modern Design:** Learn from all, create better UI/UX than competitors

### Critical Features We Must Have:

1. ⚠️ **Debt Simplification** - Users expect this (from Splitwise)
2. ⚠️ **Multi-Currency** - Essential for international users (from Tricount)
3. ⚠️ **Gamification** - Increases engagement (from OurHome)
4. ⚠️ **Offline Mode** - Important for travelers (from Tricount)
5. ⚠️ **Visual Progress** - More engaging (from Tody)
6. ⚠️ **All-in-One Integration** - Reduces switching (validated by Flatastic)

### Features to Avoid (For Now):

1. ❌ Enterprise workflows
2. ❌ Overly complex AI features (MVP phase)
3. ❌ Bank integrations (manual entry first)
