# Dream Finora - All-in-One Social Finance & Living App

## Vision

Dream Finora is a comprehensive, socially interconnected **mobile application** (iOS & Android) that integrates expense splitting, personal finance management, chore coordination, ridesharing, and community listings into a single, unified platform. Our website is a simple landing page that promotes app downloads (like Instagram, TikTok, Venmo model). Our goal is to eliminate the inconvenience of switching between multiple apps by providing one seamless mobile experience.

## Core Value Proposition

**"One app that understands your social and financial life, powered by trust scores and AI insights."**

## Key Differentiators

1. **Unified User Scoring System** - Reliability scores based on expense settlements, chore completion, and community engagement
2. **AI-Powered Features** - Advanced receipt scanning and personalized financial coaching
3. **Seamless Integration** - All features work together (rideshare expenses auto-sync to expense splitting)
4. **Multi-Currency Finance** - Local and home country finances with real-time conversion
5. **Social-First Design** - Profile-based trust system for roommates, friends, and community members
